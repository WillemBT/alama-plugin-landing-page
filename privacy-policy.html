Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blue Tern ("we", "us", "our") handles personal data when you use the Alama website and plugin.

1. Data We Collect

  • Contact data: information you provide when you email us (for example, name and email address).
  • Basic usage data: limited technical information required to operate and secure the website.
  • Support data: information you share in support requests or bug reports.

2. How We Use Data

  • To respond to inquiries and provide support.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the website and plugin.
  • To communicate product updates when requested.

3. Plugin Data Storage

In the Alama Community Edition, project data is stored locally on your machine in JSON format. Blue Tern does not automatically receive your model data from local use.

4. Legal Basis and Retention

We process personal data as needed to provide requested services, support legitimate business operations, and comply with legal obligations. We keep data only as long as necessary for these purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with trusted service providers (for example, hosting or analytics providers) only when required to operate our services.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@bluetern.eu.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email support@bluetern.eu.
