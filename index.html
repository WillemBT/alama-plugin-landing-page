The Challenge

Close the Gap Between CAD and Project Data

In complex CAD projects, model data and project documentation often drift apart. That disconnect creates avoidable rework and confusion.

Teams model in Rhino, then manually copy values into spreadsheets and notes. As soon as geometry changes, numbers go stale and decision context gets lost.

By the time certification or client review arrives, people scramble to rebuild history, validate mass budgets, and explain why key decisions were made.

Mass Data Goes Stale Fast
Parts Are Hard to Track
Decision Context Gets Lost
Our Solution

One Source of Truth Inside Rhino

Alama is a Rhino plugin built for teams that need accurate mass data and clear traceability. It combines live mass properties, part-level traceability, and documentation in one place.

When geometry changes, weight, center of gravity (COG), and mass properties update instantly. Add notes, decisions, and files directly to parts so your design history remains complete and review-ready.

From yachts and USVs to drones and complex assemblies, Alama keeps your data accurate, organized, and easy to share.

Live Mass Properties

Volume, area, centroid, and mass update instantly as geometry changes.

Part ID Traceability

Unique part IDs keep every component traceable across revisions and assemblies.

Decision Logging

Attach notes and files directly to parts so rationale stays clear and auditable.
